# Synthetic Data Assumptions & Methodology

## 1. Context & Governance
All datasets generated by Guardian are 100% synthetic, algorithmic artifacts created for the DIU CPC × upay AI Hackathon 2026. No actual customer records, real financial transactions, or production systems were accessed or replicated. 

## 2. User & Agent Population Assumptions
- **Volume**: 5,000 users and 300 agent/merchant entities.
- **Demographics**:
  - `age_group`: 18-25 (28%), 26-40 (42%), 41-60 (20%), 60+ (10%).
  - `region`: Urban (55%), Rural (45%).
  - `tenure_days`: Uniform distribution between 10 and 1,200 days.
- **Vulnerability Scoring**: A composite metric $V \in [0.0, 1.0]$ parameterized such that older age cohorts ($60+$), rural residency, and recent account creation ($<90$ days) shift the expected susceptibility upward. This reflects real-world MFS adoption curves where digital literacy gaps correlate with vulnerability to social engineering.
- **Trusted Contacts**: 60% of users possess an enrolled trusted contact capable of co-authorizing transactions.

## 3. Transactional Mechanics & Fraud Topology
- **Volume & Time Horizon**: ~150,000 transactions span a 90-day window ending October 2026.
- **Normal Patterns**:
  - Baseline expenditure adheres to a log-normal distribution ($μ=6.5, σ=0.8$, ~৳650–৳2,500 typical range).
  - Circadian variance: 85% of volume occurs between 08:00 and 22:00.
  - Cyclic boosts: Weekly salary cycle surges (Thursdays/Fridays) and month-end spikes.
- **Fraud Incidence**: Target rate calibrated between 1.8% and 2.2% across the dataset.
- **Simulated Attack Topologies**:
  1. `otp_pin_theft`: Device fingerprint mismatch within 15 minutes of a suspicious phishing message, triggering an immediate high-value transfer (>4x baseline) to drain the balance.
  2. `fake_agent_support`: Rural/elderly victim coerced into transferring 3x–8x baseline amount during irregular night hours to a newly initialized recipient.
  3. `sent_by_mistake_refund`: Victim receives an anomalous inbound transfer of ৳500–৳1,500, immediately followed by an outbound transaction of ৳3,000–৳10,000 to a different wallet.
  4. `fake_prize_fee`: Repetitive small transactions (৳200–৳1,000) disguised as lottery clearance or prize dispatch processing fees.
  5. `mule_network`: 2-to-3 hop fan-in/fan-out flows routing through clustered mule entities before cash-out.

## 4. Text Corpus & Zero-Leakage Template Splitting
- **Volume**: 6,000 messages across 6 distinct categories: `otp_pin_request`, `fake_agent_support`, `sent_by_mistake`, `fake_prize`, `urgency_threat`, and `benign`.
- **Linguistic Distribution**: Mixed Bengali script, phonetic Banglish (Latin characters), and official MFS English SMS formats.
- **Leakage Prevention**: Generated texts belong to discrete "template families". Train and test sets are split along template families rather than simple random sampling. Unseen grammatical constructions in the test set guarantee that the NLP classifier evaluates generalized scam reasoning rather than memorizing template keywords.
